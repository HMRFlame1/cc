The Game

Chaos is the objective.
Combos are everything.

Step into the boots of a foul-mouthed Aussie brawler with a short fuse and a love for destruction.

Fight through relentless waves of enemies, chaining brutal attacks and sharp-tongued insults to build massive combo scores. Earn cash through combat, unlock new weapons, adapt your playstyle, and chase the leaderboard.

This isn’t fair. It’s not polite. It’s pure arcade mayhem.

Verbally abuse everyone.

What you get

Stack hits. Stack cash. Stack chaos.

Keep the combo alive and the town doesn’t stand a chance.
